# Garden ↔ camp passage

The campground now connects to the outdoor sunny garden, rather than the atrium lobby. The four indoor lobby doors remain; the old interior camp arch is removed. Follow the right-hand stone trail north to the outdoor forest entrance. Arrive at the southern end of camp and keep walking north. Walk south through that entrance to return to the garden facing south, further down the same trail.

`src/garden-layout.js` owns the entrance, arrival, planting beds, trees and colliders. The right tree and two flowerbeds move aside for an unobstructed approach. The forest arch has no glass door wings and sits forward of the building facade. Garden camera tracking follows the visitor so the side entrance remains visible on a narrow mobile screen. Added paths and stones use the existing baked geometry and materials; no new models, textures, renderer or per-frame effects.

`protocol.js` defines garden→camp and camp→garden, with opposing doorway normals and server-owned arrival headings. `ArchiveStore` applies the canonical arrival heading; the client applies it to its own avatar on the new room epoch. Both spawn points are2.7m from the return threshold, beyond automatic trigger/rearming distance, preventing immediate bounce. Holding forward into camp continues north; holding back out continues south into the garden. Lobby→camp is rejected by server doorway validation. Direct homepage camp entry remains available.

Existing avatar identity, camp/tent geometry, seats, cafe interactions, public messages/whispers, saved notes and user data remain. Hosted `archive-live` receives the same shared layout and doorway definitions through `prepare-edge.mjs`, which now includes the garden module. Authentication, capacity, session validation, schema, RLS and secrets are unchanged. Blue Horizon is untouched.
